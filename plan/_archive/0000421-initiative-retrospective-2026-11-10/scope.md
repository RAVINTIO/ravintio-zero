# Scope - 0000421-initiative-retrospective

**Skill:** [plan](../../ravintio-zero/skills/ravintio-plan/SKILL.md)
**Feature:** 0000421-initiative-retrospective
**Version:** 0.77.1

## In Scope

- One retrospective record at `plan/retrospectives/0000360-backlog-sweep-retrospective.md`.
- One backlog entry for each unaddressed finding, or a citation of an open entry.
- A regenerated `plan/backlog/index.md`.

## Out of Scope

- Any fix to a hook, script, skill, or standard.
- Charting steps 23 onward. The coordinator does that.

## Deferred

- Each fix waits for its own step. It stays blocked until the coordinator charts the step.
