# Scope - Old-name guard fails in the full suite

**Skill:** [plan](../skills/ravintio-plan/SKILL.md)
**Feature:** 0000561-old-name-guard-fails-in-full-suite
**Version:** 0.144.0

## In Scope

- A reproduction of the full-suite failure and a named cause.
- A guard that reads a stable copy of the tree, or a fix to the earlier suite.
- A planted-change case in the guard suite.
- Docs that name the guard.

## Out of Scope

- A change to the allowlist or the old-name pattern.
- A change to the planted-name rule of 0000189 and 0000192.

## Deferred

- None.
