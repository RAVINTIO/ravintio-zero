# Scope - Step base merge regenerates the backlog index

**Skill:** [plan](../skills/ravintio-plan/SKILL.md)
**Feature:** 0000547-step-merge-regenerates-index
**Version:** 0.115.0

## In Scope

- One rule in `### I-D Advance` of the roadmap standards.
- A standards test for the rule.
- The living docs.

## Out of Scope

- Changes to `merge-queue.mjs`, `shared-files.mjs`, or `backlog-index.mjs`.
- Any hook or CI workflow change.
- A new ADR. The rule records no new decision, only a step the queue already takes.

## Deferred

Nothing deferred.
