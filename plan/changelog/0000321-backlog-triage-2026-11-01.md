## What changed

- Backlog entries carry `priority` (`high`, `medium`, or `low`) and `theme` in their frontmatter. The entry template and the filing skill name both fields.
- `ravintio-zero/scripts/backlog-index.mjs` writes `plan/backlog/index.md`, grouped by theme and ordered by priority. `--check` exits 1 when the committed index is stale. `--review` lists entries older than the review age.
- CI runs `backlog-index.mjs --check` in `.github/workflows/ravintio.yml` and in the pack copy `ravintio-zero/hooks/ravintio.yml`.
- Discovery Start Actions step 10 runs the review first and offers entries in index order. A pull-in or discard regenerates the index in the same commit.
- `backlogReviewDays` in `product.yml` sets the review age. This repo sets 5 days.
- Migration 0007 and its helpers set `priority: medium` and `theme: unsorted` on existing entries. This repo applied it and the ledger holds the row.
- Backlog entry 0000310 is pulled in and deleted. The new suites cover the five requirements.

## Why

The backlog held 59 entries with no priority, theme, or index, so pickup ran in id order and old entries went unchecked. Backlog entry 0000310 asked for a view at a glance and a pickup that starts with what matters most.

## Deviations

- Step 10 keeps its original line and adds five indented sub-bullets. A fixture test requires every line of the original text to stay verbatim.
- Real priorities and themes stay open. Every existing entry reads `medium` and `unsorted` until the human sorts them.
