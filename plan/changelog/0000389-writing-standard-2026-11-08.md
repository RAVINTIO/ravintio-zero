**Feature:** Writing standard
**Version:** set by the merge queue at merge

## What changed

- `standards/writing-standards.md` holds the British English variant of the Google developer documentation style, in a `style-rules` region and a `summary` region (0000300).
- `scripts/writing-style.mjs` prints the Claude Code output style and the Codex `AGENTS.md` section from those regions.
- `setup.sh` and `setup.ps1` write `.claude/output-styles/ravintio-writing.md` and set `outputStyle` for Claude Code. They keep one writing block in `AGENTS.md` for Codex. Both leave an `outputStyle` that is already set.
- `--no-writing-style` skips both outputs. The setup record, the flags marker, and `ravintio-refresh-setup` carry the flag.
- `formatting-standards.md` now asks for `2026-08-18` or "August 18, 2026" in body text. The em dash guard and the standard no longer suggest a semicolon.
- The Agent Dispatch Template and the driver's `--append-system-prompt` name the standard.

## Why

A developer who installs Zero had no writing standard, and `formatting-standards.md` conflicted with the British Google variant on dates and semicolons.
