**Feature:** Driver setup stop
**Version:** 0.41.0

## What changed

- `ravintio-zero/scripts/initiative-run.mjs` stops after a merged step that changes `ravintio-zero/skills/`, `ravintio-zero/hooks/`, `ravintio-zero/setup.sh`, or `ravintio-zero/setup/`. It prints `I-D Advance: Setup:`, releases the lock, and exits 1.
- A step that triggers both stops gets one `I-D Advance: Restart: Setup:` line. The file list shows 5 names, then `and {k} more`.
- The initiative skill treats the `Setup:` line as a planned stop. It runs the exact command a `Confirmation:` row names, checks every hook in `.claude/settings.json`, and starts a new driver and watcher.
- `ravintio-zero/standards/roadmap-standards.md` gets a `#### Setup stop` section. `component.yml`, `docs/architecture-overview.md`, and `docs/decisions-index.md` name the stop.
- Three new test files cover the driver, the skill text, and the standards. `test-0000250-req-002` AC-3 now changes an unwatched path.

## Why

Step sessions load the installed copies in `.claude/`. A step that changes a skill, a hook, or setup left later steps on stale copies, and the driver had no charted stop. Backlog entry 0000311 asked for it.
