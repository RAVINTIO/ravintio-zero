## What changed

- New `ravintio-zero/hooks/enforcement/block-forced-add.mjs`, a PreToolUse Bash guard. It exits 2 on `git add -f`, `--force` and its abbreviations, and any short flag group that contains `f`, including `git -C {dir} add -f`. It allows `git add plan/current`.
- `setup.sh` and `setup.ps1` wire the guard through `guard-run.mjs guard`, once, after `merge-guard`.
- The orchestrator skill forbids `git add -f` and names the fix.
- `.gitignore` lists `plan/.initiative-logs/`, `plan/.initiative-queue/`, and `plan/.initiative-runner.lock`.
- The ship skill, the pack workflow, and the tracked workflow run `git ls-files -ci --exclude-standard` and fail on a listed file.
- Two new suites cover the changes. Five older suites and one fixture now expect the extra hook entry.
- `hook-enforcement.md`, `project-operations.md`, the architecture overview, and the decisions index describe the guard.

## Why

In initiative 0000238 step 6, `git add -f plan` committed 15 driver logs, the human ack, the runner lock, and a queued entry. The push was denied and the step stopped for about four hours. Backlog entry 0000276 asked for the fix.

## Deviations

- The guard does not cover Codex. Codex wires `merge-guard` only. The CI check covers every route.
